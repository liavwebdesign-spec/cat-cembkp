01Who we are

This website is run by CAT · Crazy About Technology, [registered company name and VAT number], 5 Margarita & Pefkon Street, Athens, Greece. Email: [contact email]. We are the controller of the personal data described here.

02What we collect

Only what you send us. When you use the request form we receive your name, company, email address, shoot dates, the type of service you need and your message. If you write to us by email or WhatsApp, we receive what you write and the contact details attached to it.

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and the website does not build a profile of you.

03Why we use it

To answer your request, check availability, prepare a quote and, if we work together, run the production. The legal basis is the steps you asked us to take before a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legitimate interest in replying to business enquiries (Article 6(1)(f)).

We do not sell your details and we do not add you to a mailing list.

04Who else sees it

Form requests are stored in a Google Sheets table and in our site admin panel, and we receive an email notification for each one. These services act as our processors. Google may process data outside the EU under the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. Messages sent by WhatsApp are handled by WhatsApp (Meta) under its own terms.

Videos on this site are hosted on YouTube. They load in privacy-enhanced mode, and YouTube only receives data when you press play.

05How long we keep it

Requests that do not lead to a job are deleted after 24 months. If we work together, the records we must keep for tax and accounting are kept for the period Greek law requires.

06Your rights

You can ask to see the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, limit how we use it, receive a copy, or object to its use. Write to [contact email] and we will reply within one month.

You can also complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (dpa.gr).

07Changes

If we change this policy, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
