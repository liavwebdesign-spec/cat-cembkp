Accessibility statement

We want everyone to be able to browse the catalog and send us a request, whatever device or assistive technology they use.

Last updated: 13 October 2026

On this page

  1. Our target
  2. What we have done
  3. Known limitations
  4. Tell us
  5. Review

01Our target

This website is designed to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA.

02What we have done

  • Every page works with a keyboard alone, with a visible focus outline and a "Skip to content" link.
  • Text and buttons meet the WCAG contrast ratios on the dark background.
  • Images carry text alternatives, and decorative images are hidden from screen readers.
  • Background videos are muted and have a pause button. If your device asks for reduced motion, the videos and animations stop.
  • The request form has labelled fields and clear error messages.
  • Pages scale up to 200% zoom and adapt to phone screens.

03Known limitations

Some product spec sheets (PDF) come from the manufacturers and may not be fully accessible. If you need the information in another format, write to us and we will send it.

Videos from our YouTube channel may not have captions yet.

04Tell us

If something on the site does not work for you, email [contact email] or message us on WhatsApp. Tell us the page and what happened, and we will reply within five working days.

05Review

This statement was prepared on 13 October 2026 and will be reviewed whenever the site changes significantly.
