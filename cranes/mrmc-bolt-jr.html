Motion control

MRMC Bolt Jr

The Bolt Junior motion control system by MRMC is a compact robotic camera rig designed for high-speed precision filming.

Key specifications include:

Payload capacity: around 12 kilograms, suitable for a wide range of camera packages

Arm reach: approximately 1.2 meters, providing a good range of motion for dynamic shots3

Maximum camera lens height: up to 2 meters, allowing for flexible shooting angles

Top speed: around 3 meters per second, ideal for capturing fast-motion sequences

Repeatability: sub-millimeter accuracy, ensuring consistent, repeatable moves

It’s known for its reliability and modular design, making it a preferred choice for tabletop work, commercials, and visual effects shots

When the Bolt Junior is mounted on tracks, it gains even more versatility and range

Here’s how it works :

The track system allows the entire rig to move linearly along a set path. This gives the camera the ability to travel smoothly over longer distances, beyond the robotic arm’s fixed base reach.

Key benefits include:

1Extended camera movement: You can create long, sweeping shots or track subjects over greater distances. Enhanced precision: The track system is integrated with the motion control software, ensuring that every movement is repeatable with pinpoint accuracy. Dynamic shots: Combining the arm’s pan, tilt, and roll with the linear motion of the tracks allows for incredibly complex and cinematic camera moves. Versatility: The tracks can be laid out on the floor or even mounted vertically or overhead, offering more creative possibilities.

In essence, having the Bolt Junior on tracks transforms it from a stationary motion control system into a highly mobile platform, ideal for capturing a wide variety of dynamic shots in commercial, film, and television productions
